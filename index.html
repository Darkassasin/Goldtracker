Nepal (FENEGOSIDA)International spot in NPR

Nepal trades above the converted spot price because of import duty, taxes and dealer margin, and it often adjusts a day or two after global moves.

The forecast blends simple statistical models (linear trend, damped momentum, mean reversion, and one that follows the international spot forecast) and shows an 80% range from observed volatility. It can’t anticipate news, policy or sudden dealer-association changes, so treat it as an illustration, not financial advice. Spot gold (gold-api.com) and USD→NPR (open.er-api.com) are fetched live in your browser. The Nepal FENEGOSIDA rates and price history come from prices.json in this site’s repository, because I found no public data feed for them. The page re-checks on the schedule you choose.
